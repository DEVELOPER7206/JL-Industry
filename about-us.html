ABOUT JL INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY

Built By People Who've Actually Worked The Shop Floor.

We manufacture our own abrasives, stock every major brand, and staff our floor with reps who've actually used this stuff — not just sold it.

OUR STORY

Started On A Shop Floor, Not In A Boardroom.

JL Industrial Supply started the way most good tool suppliers do — with someone who was tired of waiting on backorders and getting talked down to by a call center. We began manufacturing our own abrasives so a stock-out on one part didn't have to stall an entire job.

Today we're a manufacturer and distributor in one — stocking every major brand alongside our own in-house line, and staffing our floor with people who've actually run this equipment, not just read the spec sheet.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Values Aren't A Poster On The Wall.

Honesty over the sale

If the part you're asking for is wrong for the job, we'll tell you before we ring it up.

Stocked, not promised

If it's on our website, it's on our shelf — not on a truck from a supplier we've never met.

Real people, real experience

Every rep on our floor has worked a shop, a jobsite, or a welding bay.

Manufacturing accountability

We make our own abrasives, so when something's wrong, you're talking to the people who built it.
