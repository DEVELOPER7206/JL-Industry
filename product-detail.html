Full Description

This zirconia alumina flap disc is built for contractors and fabricators who need consistent, aggressive stock removal without babysitting the tool. The overlapping abrasive flaps expose fresh grit continuously, so cutting performance stays consistent from the first pass to the last — instead of tapering off halfway through like a fiber disc.

Rated for use on carbon steel, stainless steel, and most ferrous metals. The fiberglass backing plate resists flex under load, keeping the disc flat against the workpiece for even material removal and a more predictable finish.

Compatible with standard 7/8″ arbor angle grinders rated for 13,300 RPM or higher. Always check your tool's max RPM rating against the disc before use.
